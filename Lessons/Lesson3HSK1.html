Third lesson HSK 1: To be (是 verb)

How to say something/someone is X:

In chinese, to say someone or something is X we use 是:

是 goes between the subject and the noun

It's easier seen than explained, let's se some examples

But first, learn these new words:

Hanzi Pinyin English
老师 lǎoshī Teacher
学生 xuéshēng Student
这 zhè this

Let's finally see another example:

我是学生 (Wǒ shì xuésheng) (I am a student)

In this case 我 is the subject, 是 to be and 学生 the noun

我 + 是 + 学生 = 我是学生 (S + 是 + N = X is Y)

Let's try with another examples

New examples:

let's see it working in some examples:

他是老师 (Tā shì lǎoshī) He is a teacher

她们是学生 (Tāmen shì xuéshēng) They are students

这是茶 (Zhè shì chá) This is tea

Notice the pattern?

VERY IMPORTANT, ONLY USE 是 WITH NOUNS, never with adjectives
