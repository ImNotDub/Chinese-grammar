First lesson HSK 1: Personal pronouns

What are the personal pronouns?

The personal pronouns are

Chinese Pinyin English
我 wǒ I / me
你 nǐ you (informal/casual)
您 nín you (formal/polite)
他 tā He / him
她 tā She / her
它 tā It (for objects/animals)
大家 dàjiā Everyone / Everybody
