First lesson HSK 1: Personal pronouns

What are the personal pronouns?

The personal pronouns are

Hanzi Pinyin English
我 wǒ I / me
你 nǐ you (informal/casual)
您 nín you (formal/polite)
他 tā He / him
她 tā She / her
它 tā It (for objects/animals)
大家 dàjiā Everyone / Everybody

See how it works!

learn these words first:

Hanzi Pinyin English
吃 chī to eat
苹果 píngguǒ apple

Let's see some examples:

Hanzi Pinyin English
我吃苹果 wǒ chī píngguǒ I eat an apple
你吃苹果 nǐ chī píngguǒ you eat an apple

Now we are going to learn 们

Pronoun + 们 = Plural pronoun, let's see how it works!

Hanzi Pinyin English
我们 wǒmen We / us
咱们 zánmen We / us (includes the person being spoken to)
你们 nǐmen you (plural)
他们 tāmen They / Them (Male-group / Gender-mixed group)
她们 tāmen They / them (Female-only group)
它们 tāmen They / them (non-human / things / animals)

let's see it working in some examples:

Hanzi Pinyin English
咱们吃苹果 zánmen chī píngguǒ We eat an apple
他们吃苹果 tāmen chī píngguǒ They eat an apple
